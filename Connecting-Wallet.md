# How To Connect Your Wallet 

   Welcome to Aura protocol, this guide will show you how to easily, seamlessly and securely connect your wallet to the decentralized application.
## Prerequisites
-  A web3 crypto wallet.
-  If you do not have a valid web3 crypto wallet click this link to download MetaMask [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/download/)*.

## How To Connect Your Wallet
1. Navigate to the Aura protocol homepage.
2. Click the **Connect wallet** button on the top right corner of your screen.
3. Select **MetaMask** for the list of supported web3 wallet providers.
4. A pop up will appear from your wallet extension. Click **approve** to sign the connection request.

>**Security Warning:** Aura protocol will never ask for your seed phrase. Only approve connection requests from official websites.
